Advertise & partnerships

Last updated: July 2026

PlugInSolarHub is the independent reference site for plug-in & balcony solar in the United States — home of the 50-state legality tracker, the savings calculator and plain-English buying guides. Our readers arrive with a purchase decision in hand: they want to know if plug-in solar is legal where they live, and which kit to buy.

Why partner with us

  • High-intent audience: readers researching legality and comparing specific kits — not casual browsing.
  • Page-1 Google rankings for state-level legality searches across a dozen states (Texas, Kansas, Wisconsin, Indiana, Georgia and more).
  • The 50-state tracker: the reference dataset for US plug-in solar rules, also available as an open dataset for developers.
  • Fast-growing market: eight states have legislated plug-in solar; New Jersey and New York are next. Every new state creates a wave of searches that already finds us first.

What we offer

  • Sponsored product review — clearly labeled, tested against the same criteria as everything else we publish.
  • Category placement — your product presented on a relevant high-intent page.
  • Newsletter feature — reach subscribers who asked to hear about kits and law changes.
  • Tracker sponsorship — category-exclusive «presented by» association with the 50-state tracker. Limited to one partner; by conversation only.

Editorial independence (read this first)

Sponsorship never buys a ranking, a score, or a place in our «best of» lists. Sponsored content is always labeled. The tracker stays accurate regardless of who sponsors it — that accuracy is exactly what makes it worth sponsoring. If your product doesn't hold up to testing, we'll tell you before we tell our readers.
