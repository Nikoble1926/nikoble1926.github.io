What is plug-in (balcony) solar?

A plug-in solar system — also called balcony solar — is one or two panels plus a small grid-tie microinverter that turns their power into household AC and feeds it through a standard wall outlet. Your home uses that free solar power first, lowering what you pull from the grid.

You can buy a complete kit (panels + inverter together) or pair a microinverter with your own panels. Germany already has 975,000+ systems installed — and with the new UL 3700 safety standard plus 2026 state laws, the U.S. is finally opening up.

Renting? It works in apartments too — no roof and no electrician needed. See our plug-in solar for renters guide.
