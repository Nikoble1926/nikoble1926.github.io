Buyer FAQ

When are plug-in solar kits cheapest?
Late spring through mid-summer is peak buying season, so Amazon and the brands often run lightning deals June through August. Prime Day 2026 ran June 23–26; the next big discount windows are typically Labor Day and Black Friday — see our Prime Day solar guide for the year-round deals tracker.
Is a complete kit cheaper than buying parts separately?
A bundled complete kit is usually cheaper for first-timers, because shipping a single box of panels + inverter + cables is more efficient than three separate orders. If you already own panels, a standalone microinverter saves more.

Is plug-in solar legal in my state?
Most states allow it under existing interconnection rules, but a handful still require utility notification. Check our 50-state legality tracker.
