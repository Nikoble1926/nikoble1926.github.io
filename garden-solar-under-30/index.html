The 3 specs that matter (30-second buyer’s guide)

  • Lumens per light, not total. Honest pathway lights are 10–30lm each. “600 lumens!” across 12 lights is marketing.
  • Replaceable NiMH battery. The battery dies in 1–2 seasons, not the light. Replaceable AA = the $25 set lasts years.
  • Real IP65 rating. Survives rain and sprinklers. No stated IP rating = skip.

FAQ

How long do cheap solar garden lights last?
The light lasts years; the NiMH battery degrades in 1–2 seasons. Buy replaceable-battery models and refresh with a $10 AA pack.
Do they work in winter or shade?
Reduced but usable in winter sun and light shade. Deep shade kills them — the panel needs ~4h of direct sun.
What should I check before buying?
Lumens per light (10–30lm honest for pathway), replaceable NiMH battery, real IP65 rating. Listings hiding all three are landfill.
