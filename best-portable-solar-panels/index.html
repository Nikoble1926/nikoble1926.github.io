Frequently asked questions

What is the best portable solar panel?

It depends on what you are powering. For a 12V fridge or recharging a power station, a 200W foldable panel like the HQST 200W is the workhorse; for a weekend with a power bank, a 100W foldable; for a phone and laptop with no battery, a 60W USB-C panel; for ultralight backpacking, a 28W USB panel.

Is a portable solar panel the same as a power station?

No. A portable panel captures sunlight; a power station stores it. A panel charges a station or a device, but on its own it only makes power while the sun is on it. See our comparison of solar versus a power station.

How many watts of solar do I need?

Phones and lights only: 20-30W USB panel. Add a camera, drone or laptop: 60-100W. Running a 12V fridge or recharging a power station: 100-200W. The honest rule: real-world output is 50-70% of the rating, so size up.

USB panels vs MC4 panels - what's the difference?

USB panels (20-60W) plug straight into phones and power banks - simplest for backpacking. MC4 panels (100-200W) connect to power stations and 12V systems - the choice for car camping, RVs and emergency home backup.
