What’s different about Prime Day 2026

  • It’s in June, not July. Amazon moved the event to June 23–26 to avoid the FIFA World Cup and the July 4th (250th anniversary) week. If you were planning a “buy in July” strategy — move it up.
  • Four full days. Like 2025, the event runs 96 hours. Lightning deals rotate constantly — the best solar discounts often appear on day 1 and day 3.
  • Prime members only. A 30-day free trial started before June 23 covers the whole event.

Prime Day FAQ

When is Amazon Prime Day 2026?
June 23–26, 2026 — four days, starting 12:01 a.m. PDT on June 23. Amazon moved it earlier than the usual July window to avoid the FIFA World Cup and July 4th week.
Do solar kits actually go on sale during Prime Day?
Yes. Microinverters, complete balcony kits and accessories regularly see 15–30% discounts, often as rotating lightning deals. EcoFlow and Anker historically run their deepest non-Black-Friday discounts during Prime events.
Do I need Prime membership for the deals?
Yes — Prime Day pricing is members-only. A 30-day free trial started before June 23 covers the entire event.
Buy now or wait for Prime Day?
It’s less than two weeks away — waiting is low-risk. Exception: if a kit is already at its lowest tracked price or showing low stock, popular models can sell out before the event even starts.
