3 setup tips that double your harvest

  • Angle beats wattage: tilt the panel at the sun and re-aim every 2 hours — flat-on-the-ground loses ~30%.
  • Heat kills output: prop panels for airflow underneath; a hot panel can lose 10–20%.
  • Charge a battery, not devices: panel → power bank/station → devices in the evening. Smoother, nothing fries.

FAQ

How many watts do I need for camping?
Phones only: 20–30W. Camera/laptop weekends: 60–100W. 12V fridge or power-station recharging: 100–200W. Real output is 50–70% of the rating — size up.
Can a panel charge a phone directly?
USB panels yes, but clouds interrupt charging. A power bank in between smooths it and stores energy for the evening.
USB vs MC4 panels?
USB (20–60W) = direct device charging, backpacking. MC4 (100–200W) = power stations, 12V systems, car camping/RV/home backup.
