The charge-speed claim, read properly

These two numbers are not measuring the same thing. Anker quotes ~49 minutes to a full 100%. EcoFlow quotes ~45 minutes to 80%. The final fifth of any lithium charge is deliberately the slowest part, so EcoFlow’s figure describes the easier half of the job. Its Amazon listing separately advertises a one-hour fast charge, which is the more comparable claim. Both are near the top of the class — but if you see “45 beats 49” written somewhere, that comparison is not sound.

FAQ

Which is better value, the Anker C1000 Gen 2 or the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Classic?
There is no winner on price any more. Checked on 25 August 2026 the DELTA 3 Classic was $499.00 on its Amazon listing (ASIN B0FQVC4RF5) and the Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 was $499.98 — 98 cents apart. Price is no longer a reason to pick either one. When we last checked on 29 July the EcoFlow was $469.00. EcoFlow’s own store showed $529.00 on the same day we read $499.00 on Amazon, so this price has moved more than once in late August — check the live listing before ordering. The Anker gives you 2,000W rated output against EcoFlow’s 1,800W, a faster full recharge, and a larger review base at 1,463 ratings versus 1,149. The EcoFlow gives you a bigger surge ceiling at 3,600W, X-Boost for appliances above the rated output, and 10ms UPS switching. Decide on those, not on price.
Do they have the same battery?
Effectively yes. Both are 1,024Wh LiFePO4 packs, which is why they compete so directly. Runtime on the same load will be near identical. Everything that separates them is in the inverter and the charging electronics rather than the cells — rated output, surge headroom, how fast they refill and how quickly they switch over when the grid drops.
Which one charges faster?
Read the two claims carefully because they measure different things. Anker states the C1000 Gen 2 reaches a full 100% in about 49 minutes. EcoFlow states the DELTA 3 Classic reaches 80% in about 45 minutes, and its Amazon listing advertises a one hour fast charge. The last 20% of a lithium charge is always the slowest part, so EcoFlow’s 45-minute figure describes an easier task than Anker’s 49-minute one. Both are genuinely fast; the Anker has the stronger claim on a like-for-like basis.
Can either run a microwave or a large appliance?
This is where they differ most. The Anker C1000 Gen 2 is rated 2,000W continuous with a 3,000W peak, so it handles more demanding loads on its rated output alone. The EcoFlow DELTA 3 Classic is rated 1,800W but has a 3,600W surge and X-Boost, which lets it drive devices rated up to 2,600W by adjusting the output waveform. If you have one specific stubborn appliance in mind, check its startup draw against both numbers before choosing.
