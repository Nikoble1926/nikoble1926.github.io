Anker vs EcoFlow (2026): Which Ecosystem?

These are the two brands most people end up choosing between, and the honest answer is that they are closer than the internet suggests. At the size most buyers actually want, the price gap is about thirty dollars, and it favours EcoFlow. What should decide it is output, how far you plan to grow, and one thing neither brand sells in America.

The charge-speed claim, read properly

These two numbers are not comparable, and both brands know it. Anker quotes ~49 minutes to a full 100%. EcoFlow quotes ~45 minutes to 80%. The final 20% of any lithium charge is deliberately the slowest part, so EcoFlow’s figure describes an easier task. That does not make EcoFlow slow — it is genuinely quick — but if you see “45 minutes beats 49 minutes” written anywhere, the person writing it did not read the footnote.

The balcony solar gap — read this before you buy

A large share of people searching “Anker vs EcoFlow” are not after a portable box at all. They want a balcony solar system with a battery: panels on a railing, a small storage unit, power fed back into the flat. In Germany that is a mature product category. In the United States it is not.

FAQ

Is Anker or EcoFlow better in 2026?
Neither brand is clearly better overall; they win on different things. At the popular 1,024Wh size the Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 was $499.98 and the EcoFlow DELTA 3 Classic was $469.00 when we checked on 29 July 2026 — a gap of under 7% - close enough that price alone should not decide it. Anker leads on rated output (2,000W with a 3,000W peak, versus 1,800W with a 3,600W surge) and on review volume. EcoFlow leads on how far the lineup scales above 2kWh and on X-Boost, which runs high-draw appliances beyond the rated output. Pick Anker for straightforward value at the entry sizes, EcoFlow if you expect to grow into whole-home backup.
Can I buy an Anker Solarbank or EcoFlow PowerStream in the United States?
No. As of 29 July 2026 neither is sold in the US. Anker SOLIX Solarbank does not appear anywhere on ankersolix.com’s US site and returns no results on Amazon US. EcoFlow PowerStream does not appear on ecoflow.com’s US site at all — the product URL redirects to the homepage. Both are European balcony-storage products, sold mainly in Germany and neighbouring markets. American buyers looking for balcony solar with storage currently have to assemble it from separate parts rather than buy an integrated system.
Which brand charges faster?
They publish the figure differently, so be careful comparing them. Anker states the SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 reaches a full 100% in about 49 minutes. EcoFlow states the DELTA 3 Classic reaches 80% in about 45 minutes. Those are not the same measurement, and the last 20% is always the slowest part of a charge. In practice both are near the top of the class and the difference will not change your day.
What does each brand actually sell in the US?
Anker SOLIX’s US range runs C1000 (1,024Wh), C2000 Gen 2 (2,048Wh), S2000 (2,010Wh), F3000, F3800 Plus and the X1 whole-home system. EcoFlow’s DELTA 3 range runs Classic (1,024Wh), Max, Max Plus, Ultra and Ultra Plus, alongside its larger DELTA Pro line. Note that EcoFlow no longer lists a model called DELTA 3 Plus in its current US lineup — if you see that name in older comparisons, the closest current equivalent is the DELTA 3 Classic.
