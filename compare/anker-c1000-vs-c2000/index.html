The pricing is linear — so what actually decides it

$499.98 → $999.99 is a $500.01 step for exactly double the battery. Anker has priced these almost perfectly proportionally, which means there is no hidden bargain on either side. You are not being rewarded for buying big, and you are not being penalised for buying small.

So the deciding factor is the one thing that is not proportional: expandability. The C2000 Gen 2 accepts a BP2000 Gen 2 expansion battery and doubles again to 4kWh. The C1000 Gen 2 accepts nothing. Buy the C1000 Gen 2 and you have bought your ceiling. Buy the C2000 Gen 2 and you have bought a starting point.

Careful — Anker sells two different 2kWh models

This trips people up constantly, and the naming does not help. The C2000 Gen 2 is not the same thing as the S2000, and they are not simply different generations of one product.

FAQ

Is the Anker SOLIX C2000 Gen 2 worth double the price of the C1000 Gen 2?
Checked on 29 July 2026 the C1000 Gen 2 was $499.98 and the C2000 Gen 2 was $999.99 — almost exactly double, for double the battery (2,048Wh vs 1,024Wh) and 400W more rated output (2,400W vs 2,000W). The pricing is close to linear, so neither is the bargain. The decision usually comes down to one feature that is not on the price tag: the C2000 Gen 2 expands to 4kWh with a BP2000 expansion battery, and the C1000 Gen 2 does not expand at all. If you may ever need more than a day of backup, that ceiling matters more than the watt-hours you get on day one.
What is the difference between the Anker S2000 and the C2000 Gen 2?
Anker sells two different 2kWh-class power stations in the US and the names do not make the difference obvious. The S2000 holds 2,010Wh but is rated 1,500W — it is built for runtime, and Anker states up to 35 hours of fridge backup. The C2000 Gen 2 holds 2,048Wh and is rated 2,400W with a 4,000W peak — it gives you both runtime and power, and it expands to 4kWh. The C2000 Gen 2 is the more capable machine; the S2000 is the cheaper way to get a large battery if you do not need heavy output. Confusingly, the S2000 is rated for less power than the much smaller C1000 Gen 2.
How much heavier is the C2000 Gen 2?
Significantly. The C2000 Gen 2 weighs 41.7 lb against the C1000 Gen 2’s 24.9 lb — about 67% more, and 16.8 lb is a real difference if you plan to carry it to a campsite rather than leave it in a basement. Anker points out the C2000 Gen 2 is 25% lighter and 29% smaller than comparable 2kWh units, which is true, but it is still nearly twice the weight of its smaller sibling.
Which one charges faster?
The smaller one, as you would expect. The C1000 Gen 2 reaches a full 100% in about 49 minutes; the C2000 Gen 2 takes about 58 minutes despite holding twice the energy, which is impressive rather than slow. On solar the C1000 Gen 2 accepts up to 600W and fills in roughly 1.8 hours, while the C2000 Gen 2 accepts up to 800W and takes about 3 hours. The C2000 Gen 2 also supports 800W alternator charging from a car, reaching 100% in about 3 hours.
