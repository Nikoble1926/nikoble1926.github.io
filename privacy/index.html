Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

PlugInSolarHub.org collects minimal data:

  • Email address — only if you voluntarily subscribe to our deal alerts via a signup form. We use double opt-in (you must confirm by email). Emails are processed by MailerLite, our email service provider, and used solely to send the alerts you asked for. Unsubscribe links are in every email.
  • Anonymous analytics — basic, aggregated page-view statistics (via Vercel Web Analytics) that do not identify you personally. We do not use advertising trackers and do not build visitor profiles.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or share your email address with third parties.
  • We don't run third-party advertising networks on this site.
  • We don't collect payment information — purchases happen on Amazon or the retailer's own site under their privacy policies.

Affiliate links & cookies

When you click an affiliate link (e.g. to Amazon), the destination site may set cookies under its own policy to attribute the referral. See our affiliate disclosure.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email, or contact us to request deletion of your data. EU/UK visitors: we honour GDPR/UK-GDPR requests; California visitors: we honour CCPA requests.
