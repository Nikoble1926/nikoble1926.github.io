Buyer FAQ

Complete kit or microinverter only?
If you want everything in one box, get the complete kit (panels + inverter). If you already have or want to choose your own panels, buy a microinverter on its own and add panels.
How much can it save per year?
A ~600-800W setup typically produces 500-900 kWh/year in good conditions, offsetting roughly $80-$270 depending on your rate. Try our calculator.
Do I need an electrician?
Most plug into a standard outlet via a safety microinverter, though some utilities want a quick notification. Check our state guide.
Is 800W a limit?
800W is the common cap many inverters follow, but U.S. rules are still forming — confirm what your utility allows.
