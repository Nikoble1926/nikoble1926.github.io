What it is

Anker SOLIX balcony solar is a modular plug-in system: high-efficiency panels, a grid-tie microinverter, and an optional Solarbank battery, all managed in Anker’s app. It’s the same family as Anker’s well-known power stations, scaled into a balcony (“Balkonkraftwerk”) product that took off in Europe.

Specs that matter (Anker’s published figures)
  • Microinverter: outputs 600W and auto-upgrades to 800W after configuration + restart; WiFi monitoring, two MPPT inputs, IP67 rating, peak efficiency around 97.3%.
  • Panels (RS40P): Anker rates them at up to ~25% efficiency (~227 W/m²), roughly 10% more output per m² than typical PERC panels — useful on a tight balcony rail.
  • Storage (Solarbank): optional LiFePO4 battery rated for 6,000 charge cycles; Anker says pairing it with the microinverter raises self-consumption from ~40% to ~90% by shifting daytime surplus to the evening.
  • Weatherproofing: panels/cables IP68, microinverter IP67.

Figures as published by Anker SOLIX. Confirm the exact SKU specs on the live listing — the lineup varies by region.

Who it’s for

Great fit: you want a polished, app-managed system, the highest panel efficiency in a small footprint, and a clean path to add storage later — and it’s in stock for you. Skip it if: you mainly want the cheapest possible bill offset, in which case a standalone UL-listed microinverter wins on value (see best 800W microinverter and the cheapest setups under $300).

The honest catch: US availability

The full Anker SOLIX balcony system (what German buyers know as a “Balkonkraftwerk”) is a European product. As of our latest check (July 28, 2026), neither Amazon US nor anker.com’s US store lists the balcony power system — only SOLIX power stations. Don’t plan around it until that changes. If it’s unavailable, the EcoFlow Stream is the closest in-stock storage-ready alternative, and a budget 800W microinverter covers the basics today.
