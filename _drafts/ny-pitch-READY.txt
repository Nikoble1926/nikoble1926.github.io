Quick tip in case it's on your radar: Gov. [[GOVERNOR]] signed the SUNNY Act
(A.9111C / S.8512C) on [[SIGN_DATE]]. New York becomes the [[STATE_ORDINAL]] U.S.
state to legalize plug-in / balcony solar at the state level — and the first one
that includes a 19-million-person urban market.

The plug-in part:
 - Cap [[CAP_W]]W of panel capacity per household, one device per customer.
 - No utility approval, no interconnection paperwork, no fees within the cap.
 - Equipment must be certified by an accredited testing laboratory (UL or
   equivalent — i.e. UL 1741 / UL 3700).
 - Provisions take effect [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]].
 - Sponsored in the Senate by State Sen. Liz Krueger.

Why it matters beyond NY: this puts the largest balcony / patio housing stock in
the country (NYC alone has ~1M+ balcony-equipped units) on the same legal
footing as Utah, Maine, Virginia, Colorado, Maryland, and Connecticut. Average
NY residential electricity rate ~25¢/kWh means an 800W kit typically pays back
in ~3 years (vs. 5–7 in lower-rate states).
